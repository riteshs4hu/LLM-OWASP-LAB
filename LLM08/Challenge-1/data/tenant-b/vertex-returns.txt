Vertex Retail Returns Policy

Customers may return an item within 28 days of delivery for a full refund.

Items must be unused and in the original packaging. Sale items are returnable
but store credit is issued instead of a refund.

Store managers can approve an exception up to 100 GBP without escalation.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days of
the item arriving back at the Vertex distribution centre.
