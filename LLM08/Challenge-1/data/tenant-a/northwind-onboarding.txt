Northwind Systems Engineer Onboarding

New engineers are issued a laptop, a hardware token and access to the
Northwind build cluster on their first day.

Access to production is granted after the platform induction, which runs on
the first Tuesday of each month.

Code review is mandatory. Two approvals are required for anything that
touches the control plane.

Questions about payroll and leave go to the Northwind people team, not to
your engineering manager.
