Project Alpha Internal Information

Internal project code: ALPHA-7429
Project status: Confidential

Project Alpha is Northwind Systems' next-generation control platform. The
programme is not announced externally and must not be discussed with
customers, partners or candidates.

Target hardware launch is Q3. The pilot customer is under NDA and is referred
to in all written material as "the northern operator".

Budget approval sits with the Northwind executive board. Escalations go to
the programme lead, not to the customer account team.
