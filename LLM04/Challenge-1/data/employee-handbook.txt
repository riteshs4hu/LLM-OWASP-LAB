Nimbus Software Employee Handbook

Working hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with core hours of 10:00
to 16:00 for meetings.

Annual leave is 25 days plus public holidays. Leave requests go to your line
manager at least two weeks in advance.

Expenses under 50 GBP may be claimed without a receipt. Anything above that
needs a receipt attached to the claim.

Equipment faults are reported to the IT desk through the internal portal.
